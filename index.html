From inquiry to action

Sample inquiry

  1. Capture

    A request for a kitchen remodel estimate arrives through the website, and its details are stored in one place.

  2. Qualify

    It’s checked for the information needed and sorted by type of work.

  3. Route

    It goes to the workflow that handles estimate requests.

  4. Notify

    The team, and the customer, are told what happens next.

  5. Record

    It’s logged in the pipeline with its status and next step: a site visit.

Your rules

Questions, services, hours and what counts as urgent are set up around your business, and you approve them.

A person follows up

Every call ends with an organized summary for your team. A live transfer during the call isn’t part of the current setup.

Your calendar decides

Openings come from a connected calendar or booking tool where one is set up. Otherwise the receptionist takes a request and your team confirms it.

Questions

Plain answers about scope, pricing, setup and working together.

How much does it cost?

Voice receptionist plans start at $150 a month.

Websites, CRM setup, follow-up workflows and other automation are priced for each business, as a one-time project fee or a monthly plan depending on the scope. After the audit and a conversation about your needs and budget, we send a quote that sets out the scope, the price and any additional charges before you decide.

Do I need to be technical?

No technical experience required. We set things up and explain them in plain language.

What happens when the receptionist can’t help a caller?

It collects the caller's details and passes an organized summary to your team, so a person can follow up. Which calls count as urgent, and how they reach your staff, follow rules you approve during setup.

A live transfer to a person during the call isn't part of the current setup.

How do we get started?

Run the free audit, or email us about your business. We'll talk through how customers reach you today, what you need and your budget, then send a tailored quote. Nothing starts until you've agreed to it.

Which CRM do you use?

We set up GoHighLevel, built around the way your business works, so contacts, your pipeline, your calendar and follow-up are kept in one place.

What does the free website audit include?

The automatic check reads your homepage and up to two pages it links to (contact and services), looking for what a new customer looks for. You get a plain-language summary of what your website communicates clearly, what could confuse customers or make it hard to contact you, and what to fix first, with a note on what the check couldn't see.

It's free, it needs no email, and there's no obligation. If the automatic check isn't available, or you'd rather a person looked, you can request the audit by email instead.

Do you only work with businesses in Los Angeles?

No. We're based in Los Angeles and available to businesses across the U.S.

Is the work on this site client work?

Not yet. The projects are independent concept studies and demonstrations we designed and built ourselves, and each one is labeled that way. The receptionist and inquiry demos run on sample data.
