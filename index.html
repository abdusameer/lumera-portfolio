01 / 03

rōk coffee and tea

Independent concept study2026

Starting questionCan the signature drink be the first thing you feel, before you read a word?

Project overview

An independent digital concept for rōk, designed to translate the ritual of a pour into a focused interactive experience. The site uses motion to create a memorable product moment while keeping the customer journey clear across desktop and mobile.

Objective

Give rōk a digital expression that feels as considered as the physical experience. The concept needed to create distinction and hold attention without letting animation compete with the information visitors need.

Lumera's response

We built a scroll-controlled pour that fills as visitors move forward and drains as they return. The interaction anchors the page and turns a familiar product ritual into a recognizable digital signature for the brand.

Project scope

Strategy · Creative Direction · UX/UI Design · Motion Design · Responsive Development

Project context

Independent concept study by Lumera Creative. Experience strategy, interface design, motion, responsive development, and supporting concept visuals were created by Lumera Creative. The storefront photographs are rōk's own. Brand names and identifying marks remain the property of their respective owners.

Fellow product study. Independent concept study by Lumera Creative. Product visualization, experience design, motion direction, and supporting concept material were developed for this study. Fellow's product designs, name, and identifying marks remain the property of Fellow.

How we built it

The pour is built as a reversible scroll sequence, so the page responds to the visitor instead of playing as a passive video. Phones receive a cropped version of the sequence that decodes faster, and the desktop and mobile compositions were tuned separately for clarity, pacing and performance.

The effect works in any window size. With reduced motion turned on, a still image replaces the sequence and no video is downloaded. A pinned locations story draws a route between the four cafés and becomes a plain list on short screens or with reduced motion. The study was tested at 21 window sizes, from 360 to 2560 px wide.

02 / 03

Lenny's Casita

Independent concept study2026

Starting questionCan a restaurant site feel like the night out it's selling?

Project overview

An independent digital concept for Lenny's Casita, designed to bring the restaurant's food, cocktails, and atmosphere into one clear online experience. Motion creates anticipation while the menu and essential visiting information remain easy to explore on every screen.

  1. Spicy Margarita Balagán
  2. Waterpamelon
  3. 616 Gimlet
  4. Carefuleta
  5. Michelada de Casita
  6. La Manzana Whiskey Sour

Objective

Present Lenny's Casita as a complete evening rather than reducing the experience to a standard restaurant menu. The concept needed to communicate the restaurant's personality, introduce the food and cocktails with intention, and help potential guests understand what makes the restaurant worth visiting.

Lumera's response

We created a chapter-based journey combining scroll-controlled film, focused food presentation, and an interactive cocktail menu that changes the visual tone of the page with each drink. Every interaction supports the restaurant's identity without making its information harder to access.

Project scope

Strategy · Creative Direction · UX/UI Design · Motion Design · Responsive Development

Project context

Independent concept study by Lumera Creative. Approved photography and published restaurant information are used with permission from Lenny's Casita. Experience strategy, interface design, motion, responsive development, and supporting concept material were created by Lumera Creative.

How we built it

The experience is built with separate desktop and mobile compositions rather than one layout shrunk across every screen. Scroll behavior, transitions, typography and color changes were tuned to keep the text readable and the page fast.

The full menu is its own page, readable without animation, hover, fullscreen mode or a specific screen size. Motion is reduced or removed when a visitor asks for it.

BB's Bakery

Independent concept study2026

Starting questionCan a small bakery's site feel handmade, like the bread?

Project overview

An independent digital concept for BB's Bakery, built around the pen-and-pencil character already present in the bakery's identity. The experience carries that visual language into a clear mobile-first journey for exploring the products, understanding the bakery's personality, and finding practical information before visiting.

Objective

Preserve the bakery's distinctive personality while making everyday customer decisions easier. The concept needed to balance expressive illustration with clear access to the products and the practical information a visitor needs.

Lumera's response

We designed the experience like a sketchbook in motion. Product drawings transition into photographic scenes as visitors scroll, while a small illustrated guide creates continuity throughout the page. The interaction encourages discovery without letting the visual concept interfere with navigation or essential information.

Project scope

Experience Strategy · Creative Direction · UX/UI Design · Illustration Direction · Motion Design · Responsive Development

Project context

Independent concept study by Lumera Creative. The experience was developed from the bakery's publicly visible identity and pen-and-pencil visual character. Experience strategy, interface design, illustration direction, motion, responsive development, and supporting concept visuals were created by Lumera Creative. The concept was not commissioned by or produced in partnership with BB's Bakery.

How we built it

The central transition connects line-drawn product states with matching photographic states. Scroll controls the transformation while the layout, typography and navigation stay put.

On phones the timing is simplified, interactions are touch-safe, and a dedicated composition replaces the desktop one, so nothing depends on hover or desktop screen dimensions. Motion follows the reduced-motion setting, and the site is prerendered so it still works without JavaScript.

Approach

We don't start with a deliverable. We start with the problem.

(04)Steps, every project
  1. 01

    Understand what's in the way.

    Before anything is designed, we find the actual obstacle: what people can't find, can't feel, or can't do.

  2. 02

    Find where technology helps.

    Sometimes it's a website. Sometimes it's a tool, an automation, or less software than you expected.

  3. 03

    Design the system that fits.

    Structure, interaction and look are designed together, for the people who will use it.

  4. 04

    Build it, and test it properly.

    Real devices, real screen sizes, keyboards and reduced motion. Then we measure and fix.
