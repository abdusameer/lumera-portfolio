Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site, and we want to hear when they can’t.

  1. Our goal
  2. What we've built in
  3. Known limitations
  4. Report a problem
  5. How we checked

01Our goal

Lumera Creative LLC uses the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA as the accessibility target for this site. That's a target we work toward, not a certification or a guarantee: nobody independent has audited the site, and we don't claim it's perfect.

02What we've built in

  • Every page scrolls the ordinary way, top to bottom. Nothing takes over scrolling or moves sideways, and no important content sits inside its own scrolling box.
  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a skip link at the top of every page.
  • Pages use real headings, landmarks, labels and descriptive link text, and meaningful images have text alternatives.
  • Text and controls are checked for contrast against their backgrounds.
  • Nothing important depends on hover. The menu on small screens and the questions on each page open and close with a tap, a click or the keyboard, and work without JavaScript.
  • Motion is short and only helps you follow the page. If your device asks for reduced motion, sections appear in place without movement, and everything is visible even if the animation doesn't run.
  • Recordings of our concept studies never play by themselves and have no sound; each has standard play and pause controls.
  • The pages work at 320 px wide and at 200% browser zoom without sideways scrolling.
  • Without JavaScript, the pages still show all their content, and the free website audit offers an email address instead of the form.

03Known limitations

  • The recordings are silent visual demonstrations of the concept studies. The text beside each one describes the work, and each study can be opened as a live site.
  • The concept studies and the receptionist demo we link to are separate websites. They aren't covered by this statement and may not meet the same standard.
  • The automatic website audit uses Cloudflare Turnstile to confirm a person started it. If that check is hard to complete, use "Prefer a person to look? Request an audit by email" on the same form, or email us.

04Report a problem

If something on this site is hard or impossible to use, tell us at contact@lumeracreative.agency. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and, if you can, the device and assistive technology you use. We read every report and will reply.

05How we checked

We last reviewed the site on October 28, 2026. We used automated checks (axe-core, and our own browser tests in Chrome), keyboard-only navigation, focus and reduced-motion checks, and a review of every page at phone, tablet and desktop sizes from 320 px to 1920 px wide. We haven't yet tested the site with screen readers, in other browsers or on a range of physical devices, and it hasn't been reviewed by an independent auditor.
