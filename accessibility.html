Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to use this site, and we want to hear when they can't.

  1. Our goal
  2. What we've built in

  4. Report a problem

01Our goal

We aim for this site to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. That's a target we work toward, not a certification: nobody independent has audited the site, and we don't claim it's perfect.

02What we've built in

  • Everything works with a keyboard, with a visible focus outline and a skip link at the top of every page.
  • Pages use real headings, landmarks, labels and descriptive link text, and meaningful images have text alternatives.
  • Text and controls are checked for contrast against their backgrounds, including on each project's color.
  • Every screen recording has a pause and play button, and nothing plays with sound.
  • If your device asks for reduced motion, the sideways scrolling and scroll-driven movement are replaced by a plain stacked page, recordings don't start by themselves, and the final state of every effect is shown.
  • The page works at 320 px wide and at 200% browser zoom without sideways scrolling, and in small or non-fullscreen windows.
  • Without JavaScript, the pages still show their content, with still images in place of recordings.

03Known limitations

  • On large screens, and on phones held upright, the opening and the project strip move sideways as you scroll. A reduced-motion setting, a phone held sideways or a small window gives you the stacked page instead.
  • Text size follows the window width and doesn't follow the default font size set in a browser. Browser zoom works.
  • The recordings are silent visual demonstrations of the concept studies. The text beside each one describes the work, and each study can be opened as a live site.
  • Scroll it yourself shows a separate concept website inside a screen. Each study is its own site, and the studies we link to open as separate sites; they aren't covered by this statement and may not meet the same standard.

04Report a problem

If something on this site is hard or impossible to use, tell us at lumera@lumeracreative.com. Tell us the page, what you were trying to do and, if you can, the device and assistive technology you use. We read every report and will reply.

05How we checked

We last reviewed the site on October 5, 2026. We used automated checks (axe, Lighthouse and our own browser tests at widths from 320 to 1440 px), keyboard-only navigation, focus and contrast checks, and a review of the accessibility tree. The site has not yet been tested with a range of screen readers or reviewed by an independent auditor.
