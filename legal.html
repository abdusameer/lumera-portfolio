03Concept studies and what belongs to others

All the projects are independent concept studies and capability demonstrations by Lumera Creative LLC, made on our own initiative, not commissioned or paid client work. The concept studies are redesigns of real businesses. Three projects have their own pages (rōk coffee and tea, Voice Receptionist and BB's Bakery); the others, Lenny's Casita, Stagger Coffee, Motiq and Tirzahs Mexi-Terranean Grill, are linked from the Work page. None of the concept studies is an official website of the business, and none was commissioned, sponsored or endorsed by it. Voice Receptionist is an internal capability demonstration: its businesses, callers and bookings are fictional sample data, and it does not place calls, create bookings or submit orders. The Fellow product concept within the rōk study is likewise independent: Fellow did not commission, approve or sponsor it.

All third-party names, trademarks, product designs, and identifying marks remain the property of their respective owners and are used only to identify the subject of an independent concept study. A business's name, products, buildings, menus or marks appearing inside our media doesn't mean that business commissioned, approved, sponsored or endorsed the concept. Lenny's Casita has given us permission to use its approved photography and restaurant information in its concept. The other original imagery and video was created by Lumera Creative LLC for the studies; where it appears it is labeled Concept image or described as a concept, and it is not official photography of the business. Credits are listed with each project.

Menus, prices, hours and addresses shown in the studies may be out of date. For current details, please check with the businesses directly.
