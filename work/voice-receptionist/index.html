Voice Receptionist

Can a business owner see what a voice receptionist would do before trusting it with a call?

What
Interactive demonstration
Businesses
Salon · HVAC · Plumbing · Restaurant
Data
Fictional sample data; it connects to nothing

The idea

A voice receptionist demonstration showing how customer conversations can be organized into clear business requests. Visitors choose a business type and follow a sample call through a scroll-controlled story.

Each business asks different questions, collects different details and ends with a different result: an appointment request for a salon, a service request for an HVAC company or a plumber, and a reservation or pickup order for a restaurant.

The demonstration also shows what changes from one business to the next (services, scheduling rules, questions, capacity, menu options and when a person should take over) and what stays the same in every call.
