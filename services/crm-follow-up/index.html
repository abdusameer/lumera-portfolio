A sample inquiry, start to finish

A scripted example with fictional data. It isn’t connected to a real customer, inbox, CRM or production system.

  1. Capture

    Alex Morgan asks for a kitchen remodel estimate through the website. The details are stored in one place.

  2. Qualify

    The request is checked for the information needed and sorted as an estimate request.

  3. Route

    It’s assigned to the residential estimates workflow and the estimates team.

  4. Notify

    The team gets a new-request alert, and the customer an acknowledgement.

  5. Record

    It’s logged in the pipeline as New, with the next step: a site visit.
